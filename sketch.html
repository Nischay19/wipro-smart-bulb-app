Glow: interactive sketch

Mockup of the macOS menu-bar panel. Everything here is clickable; the room preview shows roughly what the bulb would do.

Layout decisions

  • Warm White is the hero. Top of the panel, biggest card, starred My Glow preset (whitish yellow ≈ 3080 K, 85%).
  • Colour: drag the wheel for any hue/saturation, or tap one of 16 swatches.
  • Natural Light: one switch; the line underneath says what it's doing right now.
  • Any manual change pauses Natural Light (same as Philips Hue).

What each control sends

  • Power → DP 20
  • White → DP 21 = white, DP 22 brightness, DP 23 warmth
  • Colour → DP 21 = colour, DP 24 = hhhhssssvvvv
